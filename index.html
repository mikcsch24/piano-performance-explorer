Data analytics case study

Piano Performance Explorer

How do two piano tracks differ across a performance? I used SQLite to analyze 941 notes and Streamlit to explore timing, pitch, and dynamics.

Where the performance gets busiest

Track 1 contains 540 notes and averages 0.631 seconds per note. Track 2 contains 401 notes and averages 0.446 seconds per note.

From MIDI to insight

I exported note-level data from EditMIDI, imported the CSV into SQLite, and grouped notes by track and time interval. The Streamlit app uses the saved SQL queries to build the charts.
